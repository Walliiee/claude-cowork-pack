# Weekly report

Needs: the Google Drive connector, and the name or link of the sales sheet.

1. Find the sales sheet in Drive. If there is more than one candidate, ask which one.
2. Compare this week with last week. List deals that are new, won, lost, or moved to a later date.
3. Flag anything that slipped: a later close date, a lower value, or no update for two weeks.
4. Write a one-page report: summary at the top, then the slipped deals, then everything else.
5. Make five slides from the report: overview, wins, slipped deals, next week, open questions.
6. Say where both files are saved.

## Rules

- Use only figures from the sheet. If a number is missing, write "missing" instead of estimating.
- Do not edit the sales sheet.
- Do not send or share the report. The person who asked does that.
