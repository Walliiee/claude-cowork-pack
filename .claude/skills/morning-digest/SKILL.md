# Morning digest

Needs: the Slack connector, and the list of channels to read.

1. Read messages posted since the last digest. On Monday, include the weekend.
2. Group them by channel.
3. Pull out three kinds of item: decisions made, questions waiting for an answer, and anything that mentions the reader by name.
4. Write the digest. Decisions and open questions first, then one line per channel for the rest. Link each item to its message.
5. If a channel had nothing new, leave it out.

## Rules

- Keep it to one screen. Cut chatter and reactions.
- Quote people exactly when the wording matters. Do not guess what they meant.
- Do not post, reply or react in Slack. The digest goes to the reader only.
