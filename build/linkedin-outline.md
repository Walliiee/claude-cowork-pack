# LinkedIn carousel outline (approved 14 Oct 2026; use-case slides trimmed to 25 words max)

Format: 10 slides, 1080x1350 (4:5). Colours and fonts as the guide: plum #451D3E, cream #F7E7D0, orange #F89404, sand #F0DCBE; Fraunces headings, Inter body, DM Mono for prompts.
"Body" is the text on the slide. "Layout" and "Image" are production notes and do not appear on the slide.
No time-saved numbers anywhere: none of the official sources gives one for these tasks.

## Slide 1: Cover

Body:
> Hand Claude the task. Get the file back.
> Krohn AI · mikekrohn.ai

Layout: plum background. Hook in Fraunces, large, top half. Brand line small at the bottom left.
Image: slot, 1080x1350 hero behind the lower half (e.g. a desk with a laptop showing a finished report). Plum overlay so the hook stays readable.

## Slide 2: Chat vs Cowork in one line

Body:
> Chat: you ask, it answers.
> Cowork: you hand over a task, you get files back.
> Now: one Claude does both.

Layout: three stacked horizontal bands. Band 1 cream with a speech-bubble icon, band 2 sand with a folder-and-file icon, band 3 orange with both icons joined by a plus sign. One line per band, large type, no other text.
Image: none; icons only.
Sources: [1] merge and "one Claude"; [2] "hand it back as finished files you can edit".

## Slide 3: The weekly report you stop writing

Body:
> Input: sales sheet in Drive
> Claude: flags what slipped, drafts slides
> You get: report and deck, every Monday
> "Summarise last week's pipeline. Five slides."

Layout: big title top. Three-step flow as three rounded boxes joined by arrows, labelled Input > Claude > You get. Prompt in a DM Mono box at the bottom. Same template for slides 3 to 7.
Image: slot, 4:5 panel behind the title, top third (a slide deck thumbnail).
Tag: small "beta" pill next to the title (Claude Slides is in beta).
Features and sources: connectors (Drive) [2]; scheduled tasks, weekly [2][4]; Claude Slides, beta on paid plans [1][2]. The example follows Anthropic's own weekly-report scenario [1].

## Slide 4: Your morning digest, before you sit down

Body:
> Input: team Slack channels
> Claude: reads new messages each weekday
> You get: one short summary
> "Weekdays at 8 AM: summarise new Slack messages."

Layout: same three-box flow as slide 3. Small clock icon next to the title.
Image: slot, 4:5 panel (a phone showing a short summary).
Features and sources: connectors (Slack, Gmail, Microsoft 365) [2]; scheduled tasks that run in the cloud while your computer is off [2][4]. The prompt shortens Anthropic's example "Every weekday at 8 AM, summarize new messages in my team's Slack channels" [2].

## Slide 5: Compare tools, with sources attached

Body:
> Input: your requirements
> Claude: searches the web, compares options
> You get: spreadsheet plus one-page summary
> "Compare five project tools. Source links."

Layout: three-box flow. Under it, a mock spreadsheet strip of three rows as a visual hint.
Image: slot, 4:5 panel (a spreadsheet close-up).
Features and sources: web search during a task [2]; spreadsheets with working formulas [2]; keeps running after you close the laptop [1][2]. Based on Anthropic's example "Research the top project management tools for small teams" [2].

## Slide 6: Clean up the shared folder

Body:
> Input: one folder on your computer
> Claude: sorts files by type and date
> You get: a tidy folder
> "Sort this folder by type and date."

Layout: three-box flow. Left box shows a messy file icon pile, right box a neat folder tree. Deletion prompt is covered on slide 9.
Image: slot, 4:5 panel (before and after folder view).
Features and sources: folder access in Claude Desktop, read, organise and create files [2][3]; asks before permanently deleting files by default [2]. Prompt adapted from Anthropic's "Organize my Downloads folder by file type and date" [2].

## Slide 7: From notes to a deck you can present

Body:
> Input: interview notes
> Claude: pulls themes and quotes
> You get: a doc and a 10-slide deck
> "Top themes, one quote each, 10 slides."

Layout: three-box flow. Right box shows a doc page and a slide side by side.
Image: slot, 1080x1350 hero (a presenter screen), cropped to the lower half.
Tag: small "beta" pill next to the title (Claude Docs and Slides are in beta).
Features and sources: reads your files [2]; Claude Docs and Claude Slides, beta on paid plans; download as PowerPoint or PDF [1][2]. Prompt combines two Anthropic examples [2].

## Slide 8: Set it up in 20 minutes

Body:
> 1. Write your instructions for Claude
> 2. Connect two or three apps
> 3. Make one working folder
> 4. Brief one real task
> 5. Schedule one weekly report

Layout: vertical checklist, five rows. Each row: a round orange icon (person, plug, folder, speech bubble, calendar) and one line of text.
Image: none; icons only.
Sources: Instructions for Claude in Settings > General [2]; connectors under Customize > Connectors [2]; folder access [2][3]; briefing with outcome, format and inputs [2]; scheduled tasks [4].

## Slide 9: Four safety habits

Body:
> Start on Manual: Claude asks first.
> Give it the smallest folder.
> Keep originals out of reach.
> Review anything that sends or deletes.

Layout: four short lines, each with a small plum tick. Cream background, orange top bar.
Image: none.
Sources: Manual is the default, Auto is opt-in [2]; review work with real consequences such as sending messages or changing files [2]; asks before permanent deletes [2].
